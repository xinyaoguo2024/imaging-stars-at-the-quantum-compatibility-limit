# Fig. 5 black-hole mass audit

Selection rule: prefer direct maser, gas/stellar dynamical, or reverberation-mapping measurements. Use host-galaxy scaling relations only where no direct measurement is available. The quoted uncertainties are heterogeneous and retain the convention of the selected source paper.

| Source | Adopted mass | Method and reason |
|---|---:|---|
| NGC 1068 | \((16.6\pm0.1)\times10^6 M_\odot\) | Revised, nearly edge-on water-megamaser disk dynamics (Gallimore et al. 2024). |
| NGC 4151 | \(4.57^{+0.57}_{-0.47}\times10^7 M_\odot\) | Reverberation mapping (Bentz et al. 2006), consistent with independent stellar-dynamical estimates. |
| NGC 1275 | \(1.1^{+0.9}_{-0.5}\times10^9 M_\odot\) | Stellar-dispersion estimate consistent with molecular-gas dynamics (Riffel et al. 2020). |
| Mrk 421 | \(\log_{10}(M_\bullet/M_\odot)=8.28\pm0.11\) | Host stellar velocity dispersion (Barth et al. 2003). |
| Mrk 501 | \(\log_{10}(M_\bullet/M_\odot)=9.21\pm0.13\) | Host stellar velocity dispersion (Barth et al. 2003). |
| BL Lac | \(\log_{10}(M_\bullet/M_\odot)=8.23\pm0.30\) | Host stellar velocity dispersion, with conservative intrinsic scatter (Woo & Urry 2002). |
| 3C 273 | \((2.6\pm1.1)\times10^8 M_\odot\) | Spatially resolved BLR dynamics (GRAVITY Collaboration 2018). This replaces the higher, more model-dependent SARM value used previously. |
| OJ 287 | \(1.84\times10^{10}M_\odot\) (1% adopted uncertainty) | Standard relativistic binary-orbit solution (Valtonen et al. 2012). This value remains explicitly model dependent. |
| PKS 1510-089 | \(5.71^{+0.62}_{-0.58}\times10^7 M_\odot\) | Spectroscopic reverberation mapping (Rakshit 2020). |
| 3C 279 | \(\log_{10}(M_\bullet/M_\odot)=8.90\pm0.50\) | Host-bulge luminosity relation (Nilsson et al. 2009); no comparably direct dynamical mass is available. |

The active calculation uses \(T\Delta f=10^{11}\). All source-specific baselines and label uncertainties are regenerated from these masses rather than rescaled by hand.
